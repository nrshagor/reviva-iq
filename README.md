# RevivaIQ Base - Sales Recovery & Core Insights for Odoo 18

Created and maintained by Noore Rabbi Shagor under CodeNRS.

RevivaIQ Base is a production-ready Odoo 18 analytics module focused on sales recovery, dead stock intelligence, inactive customer monitoring, and executive operational insights.

Designed for growing businesses, RevivaIQ helps organizations identify inventory risks, customer inactivity, and revenue recovery opportunities through a clean operational dashboard and analytics workflow.

---

# Features

## Executive Dashboard

- Dead stock monitoring summary
- Customer recovery analytics summary
- Revenue risk overview
- Analytics snapshot tracking
- One-click navigation to detailed operational records

## Dead Stock Intelligence

- Detect products with no recent sales activity
- Risk scoring and risk level classification
- Inventory risk value tracking
- Operational workflow:
  - Draft
  - Active
  - Reviewed
  - Resolved

- Safe regeneration protection for reviewed/resolved records

## Customer Recovery Intelligence

- Detect inactive customers
- Recovery score engine
- Customer inactivity classification
- Recovery workflow:
  - New
  - Review
  - Contacted
  - Recovered
  - Ignored

## Analytics Snapshot Engine

- Generate operational analytics snapshots
- Revenue risk summaries
- Historical analytics tracking
- Snapshot confirmation workflow

## Operational Productivity

- Advanced search filters
- Group-by analytics
- Multi-company ready architecture
- Performance-safe analytics limits
- Batch-safe processing design

# Installation

2. Restart Odoo.

3. Update Apps List.

4. Install:
   - RevivaIQ Base
